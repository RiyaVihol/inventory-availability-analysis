Inventory Availability & Synchronisation Improvement

Business Analyst Portfolio Project

A case-study project focused on improving inventory accuracy and product availability across online and physical stores.

 1. Business Problem

The current inventory system does not update product availability accurately across sales channels. Customers may therefore place orders for products that are actually out of stock, resulting in cancellations and customer dissatisfaction.

 2. Business Objectives

- Improve inventory accuracy across online and physical stores.
- Reduce orders placed for unavailable products.
- Reduce inventory-related order cancellations.
- Improve customer experience by providing reliable availability information.

5. Key Business Findings

- S03 has the highest inventory discrepancy:15 out of 22 total discrepancy units (~68%).
- 4K Monitor and Tablet Air are the biggest discrepancy contributors: together 18 out of 22 (~82%) of total discrepancy.
- S03 is the first investigation priority, particularly for 4K Monitor and Tablet Air.
- Wireless Mouse has the highest order quantity, but it is not one of the major discrepancy products.
- High order volume does not automatically mean high inventory discrepancy.
- In the analysed data, all 5 cancelled orders were associated with an out-of-stock reason.
- S03 also has the highest data-error count (3), strengthening the case for investigating its inventory records/processes.

6. Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory reconciliation at S03.
- Investigate 4K Monitor and Tablet Air first.
- Review inventory update and synchronization processes at S03.
- Compare system inventory records with physical stock for affected product-store combinations.
- Improve inventory availability synchronization so customers see accurate stock information before ordering.
- Monitor inventory-related cancellations and discrepancies after implementation.
